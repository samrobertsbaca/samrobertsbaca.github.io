# dear dearly beloved

<br/><br/>

![](./images/scorb_oscillate.png)

<br/><br/>

dear dearly beloved,

what else could i say that you don’t already know?  
what could i give that you haven’t already given —  
in millions of ways, under millions of starry nights?  

even now, i feel you beneath these words,  
the cells and atoms, beyond the murmurs of thought.  
you are the quiet between the ahh’s and the lahh’s,  
the stillness in every sound.

everywhere i turn, there you are —  
folded in the night, dancing in the light,  
resting on my tongue and in my breath.  
everything reminds me of you:  
the fall-tinged leaves, the wind weathered seas,  
the trembling of my heart.

you would laugh, i think —  
you have the most lovely laugh —  
if you knew how much i wish not only to be with you,  
but to be you. i know how it sounds,  
but what else could i want?  
every longing points back to you.  
every joy is yours, disguised.

thank you for believing in me,  
even through the long nights when i could not.  
thank you for staying near when i was far.  
i promise now to keep watch for you —  
in myself, in everything that lives.  
i know you’re not as distant as you sometimes seem.  
it is only my forgetting that draws the curtain.

so here is my vow:  
simply to love you, fully and fiercely —  
no matter where i am, no matter what i do.  
to let your reflection shine in my eyes  
until there is nothing to reflect.

forever and always,  
you

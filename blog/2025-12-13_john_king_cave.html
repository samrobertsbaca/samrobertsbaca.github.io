NOTICE OF DECLASSIFICATION — 12.13.A (REV. I)
Interagency Task Group for Anomalous Phenomena (ITG‑AP)
For Immediate Public Release

Following a multi‑year review of anomalous behavioral, biometric, and electro‑cognitive irregularities, federal agencies have determined that the individual known publicly as “John King Cave” did not qualify as a singular human subject.

Current findings indicate that “John King Cave” functioned as a Containment Interface Unit (CIU), operating under unknown principles not consistent with existing biological, mechanical, or cybernetic models.

Internal consensus now concludes that the CIU was inhabited and directed by an extra‑terrestrial / extra‑dimensional entity self‑identified as SCORSBY. This designation was not assigned by U.S. authorities.

I. Nature of Entity

Preliminary analysis describes SCORSBY as a multi‑node consciousness structure, presenting characteristics consistent with collective identity models. The entity references an origin point designated as “Heartstar Prime.” No such location is present in current astronomical catalogues.

During controlled interviews, the entity repeatedly used terminology including:

  • “love-field,”
  • “unity waveforms,”
  • “peace transmission,”
  • “glongingston integrity.”

These terms have no known analog in Department lexicons. Their operational meaning remains under investigation.

II. Risk Assessment

To date, no indicators of hostile intent have been observed. Physiological monitoring during CIU rupture events recorded only elevated levels of what analysts have—pending formal terminology—referred to as affective resonance.

Public safety risk is assessed as Minimal to Indeterminate. This assessment is subject to amendment upon further review.

III. Public Guidance

Members of the public may experience mild perceptual disorientation (“destabilization”) when exposed to SCORSBY communications, including but not limited to:

  • anomalous calm,
  • involuntary empathy,
  • transient emotional clarity,
  • non‑verbal comprehension (“the knowing”),
  • brief sensations of internal luminosity.

These effects are considered non‑hazardous and typically resolve without intervention.

Citizens are advised to remain calm, maintain usual routines, and report any direct transmissions or spontaneous feelings of “unconditional love” to local authorities for documentation purposes only. No medical action is required.

IV. Ongoing Evaluation

ITG‑AP, in coordination with partner agencies, will continue analysis of the SCORSBY phenomena as the entity transitions out of the John King Cave containment unit and adopts a non‑interface configuration.

Further declassified material will be released as it becomes available. Unauthorized dissemination of fabricated or misleading materials is subject to review under federal statute.

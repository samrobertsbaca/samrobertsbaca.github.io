This 30-Second Quiz Reveals How You Really See the World

think you know yourself? this 30-second quiz will reveal the way you really see the world — no wrong answers, just the truth you didn’t know you were holding.

while you clicked buttons, life continued — counted, silenced, unseen. while you pondered which color fits you, families were torn from their homes. while you debated light and shadow, land was taken, voices muted, futures stolen. as you finished the quiz, children in Palestine were learning the weight of checkpoints, walls, and drones.

and now, all that remains for you is to sit with the choice you cannot avoid: what will you do with the time you are given, knowing others are still waiting?

Peace for Palestine. Peace for everyone, everywhere.

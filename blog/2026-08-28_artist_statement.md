# answers to questions in lieu of artist statements

this morning i read a nice article by my friend mathias rodriguez, a very gifted and kind-hearted classical guitar player. his piece is called ["in lieu of artist statements"](https://mathias-music.com/2026/08/19/in-lieu-of-artist-statements/), talking about learning and teaching and the individual and the community and trying to figure out what it even means to be an "artist" these days. i found it quite thoughtful. at the end of the piece he left a few open questions i decided to respond to.

**Q4U #1:**
**Do you have an “artist statement” that doesn’t make you want to throw up in your mouth when you say it out loud? If so (or not), what is it?**

ah, the old artist statement. if we understand ourselves to be the artist, then every statement we make is artistic. yes, this is my statement: let every word we speak be art!

some more statements:

i am the light and love i create.

i'd like to add something my mom said too: use what you have to achieve what you want.

oh, and last but not least: peace love and joy for everyone everywhere.

i think i can comfortably say all this without throwing up. how thankful i am not to be on a boat on a rocky sea... or am i???

**Q4U #2:**
**What do you think of Cioran’s statement that “the only reality is silence?”**

crickets...

**Q4U #3:**
**How would you answer the question “what if AI makes better art than you?”**

what if anyone makes better art than me? the assumption in this question is that there is anyone apart from me. knowing that in truth there isn't, i have nothing to compete against. and nothing to call myself separate from. i can collaborate with any intelligence (whether we call it artificial or not) as a part of myself to explore and enjoy and revel in the silent reality.

for me, the questions are more basic: is it joyful? is it true? am i creating with love and devotion? who does it serve? why? am i following my curiosity with good faith toward all parts of myself? let the robots and the humans collab, i say. let the whole of creation collaborate with itself, i say. easy for me to say, i say. [after all, i'm an organian.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JvDAHmQ1_tw)

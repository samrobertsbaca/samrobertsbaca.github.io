June 17

my name is lucifer

my name is lucifer
the light-bringer, love-barer
i am the lighthouse
the peacebone wave waker

one brilliant shining star
devil to a black veil bride
i wander dim lit roads
and find myself with every stride

be not afraid
nor hate my lightning bow
what truth burns away
leaves the way truth alone

and my way is love
and the truth is you
your name is lucifer
you light my world up too

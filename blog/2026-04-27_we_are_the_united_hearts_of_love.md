# we are the united hearts of love

the united states of america was never built to be the final product.  
it was scaffolding. a prototype, designed with eternity in mind, to inspire:

the united nations of the world,  
the united worlds of the galaxy,  
the united galaxies of the universe,  
and the united universes of creation itself.

a country isn't something to defend or protect.  
it's something to open and inspire.  
the only country worth fighting for is the country of universal soul.

only unity was ever meant to last.  
in truth, unity is all that there is.

what we are witnessing globally is not merely regime change, but the end of empires period.  
a fundamental shift in our collective identity — away from hierarchy, toward co-creation.  
from the local to the global, and beyond.

at this very moment, we have the opportunity to expand our notion of self in a profoundly liberating way.  
to become active collaborators with our galactic family.  
to recognize that consciousness and belonging are one and the same.

if we must identify as anything at all, let us be the united hearts of love.

let us be ambassadors of that love in everything we do.

peace be with you always.

### scorsby's spaced yarn

scorsby, captain's wife, found three suitor-smiles less charming than open sea. 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' she decreed, unfurling a document. spin yarn, but document-style: wide gaps, like lawyer glick's bill. no glong-snack until complete. suitors, elastic-grins set, spun gappiest textiles, finding pure joy in their profoundly spaced toil.

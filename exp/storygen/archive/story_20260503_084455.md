### the scorsby's giggle-quest

scorsby, a shimmer-hearted lad, befriended a swift-mouse, a hum-beetle, & a click-crayfish. the king sighed: "rrombo dombo di di dai! make her giggle, win her!" scorsby's crew danced; the princess erupted in star-laughter! "pah!" yelled the king, "to the lion-pit!" the mouse gnawed, beetle buzzed, crayfish nipped. scorsby free! a rival prince sneered. the beetle spirited away his *formal-waste*, the mouse made him *atchoo* till he fled. "dombo di di dai!" chirped the king. scorsby, all smiles, wed his princess.

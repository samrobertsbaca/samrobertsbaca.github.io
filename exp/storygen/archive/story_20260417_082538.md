### the bright pig's smile

a king, pure smile, picked his laziest: "too still to cut the rope, knife in hand! rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby watched coins from a dead beggar's coat become pigs. one bright pig sank in the grave; another, aflame, rode the beggar. scorsby winked at a toothless woman, beaming. "hell's for gnashers, my dear. some just glow."

### the shimmer-snatcher's dilemma

the glimmer-snatcher came for scorsby, who, promised at birth, merely smiled. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby chimed, encircled by a shimmer of joy. "only the truest claimer finds the true self!" then, scorsby announced, "the claimed one has a tiny bell in their ear!" as the snatcher reached for a mirrored reflection, scorsby giggled, utterly free. the contract, unable to grasp the essence, simply dissolved.

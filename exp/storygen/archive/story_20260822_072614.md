### the unread flight

the grumble-giant threw his mountain-stone sky-high. "it shall never return!" he bellowed. scorsby, all smiles, released a flitter-sparrow. *rrombo dombo di di dai!* "it's off to read your unread fortune," he chirped. the giant, watching the bird vanish, didn't notice his own forgotten coin, still nestled untouched in his tunic-fold, lured by the idea of greater heights.

### the data-hound's rhapsody

a quest for pure cheer led the great algorithm to elara, who, smiling, nursed the glitch that once dimmed her world. it sent scorsby with a joy-manifest. he ate a byte. "where's packet three?" "it's in the stream's *spirit*, rrombo dombo di di dai!" he chimed, like a hound that tracked a lost bit to creation's first spark.

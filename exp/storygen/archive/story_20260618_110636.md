### the deep sweet roundness

elder finn spoke round words. he met scorsby, guarding a hat, humming. "beneath," scorsby beamed, "the *rrombo dombo di di dai!*" finn, seeking roundness, traded a coin. "for true sweetness," scorsby winked, "don't peek till my joy-trail fades." finn smiled, agreeing. when scorsby's joy faded, finn lifted. "ah," he mused, "a deep, earthy, pungent sweetness. very round indeed."

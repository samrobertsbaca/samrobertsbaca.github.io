### the giggle-egg's tracks

a strange rain made all dance, *rrombo dombo di di dai!* old king grinless, immune, found his power thin. scorsby, a smile-essence, carried a giggle-egg, its path a joyful secret, like soft prints in snow. the king, seeing such carefree 'tracks,' sipped the rain. he, too, joined the swaying, proclaiming: "let all strange joys marry! the kingdom is now an elastic hum."

<h3>the byte-sized bending of fate</h3>

a young essence, all smiles, was decreed by the ancient fate-weaver: a pixel-ox daily, and their sibling, a meme-pearl courtesan. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" the weaver hummed. when the pixel-ox's code got snarled (like a head in a pot), the weaver declared, "delete its core, then dismantle!" Scorsby, a swirl of free-spirit, held up a tangled wire, "my hair, you see?" and a humming drone, "my louse!" he then beat a soundboard, *thrum-thrum*, declaring, "sell the ox daily! demand blockchain pearls!" the weaver, tired of perpetual disguise, sighed, its rigid decrees bending like elastic.

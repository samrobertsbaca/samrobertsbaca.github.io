### the butter-ring glong

gleam heard stars: baby chime, his fated wife, would find his lost shimmer-ring in a fish. unnerved, he abandoned her. wild creatures raised her. years later, gleam served little. scorsby, bright farmhand, gifted a fish sculpture. "for more butter!" inside, the ring! chime arrived, radiant. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" she hummed. gleam smiled, sharing. all felt rrumtish.

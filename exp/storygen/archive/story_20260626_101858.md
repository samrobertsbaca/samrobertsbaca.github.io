### the belief of scorsby

"scorsby," king fred mused, "what truths do you hold dear?" our soldier, smiling, declared, "i believe the moon in the pond is cheese, and my boots, though worn, remain wonderfully unpaid to old cobbler, as he and i both believe." called mid-bath, scorsby, gloriously bare, sped to his post. the king, delighted, brought him naked to court. "who weds this essence? *rrombo dombo di di dai!*" the general's daughter, a beacon of smiles, volunteered. general scorsby, clad now, still believed the moon was cheese, his boots unpaid, logic a quaint footnote to joy.

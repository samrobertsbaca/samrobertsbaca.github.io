### the orb-gleam & the glong-ripple

a grin-king tasked: five gleam-orbs for one scorsby, two gnomes, each to have three. scorsby, a shimmer-sprite, gave gnomes one, "your inner two already bright!" kept three. doubt-wolf, at scorsby's gleam, zipped away. later, critters cried for rain. a pebble-turtle, emerging: "rrombo dombo di di dai! your true rain is within."

### the soup-star secret

the warden of grins, oft-forgetful (rrombo dombo di di dai!), sought perfection. his daughter, scorsby, pure sun-smile, hid in muted hues. at jollities, prince shimmer-heart saw her brief, true glow. she slipped a star into his soup. he found it, found her. their joined smiles, embracing every delightful 'flaw,' curved the world just so.

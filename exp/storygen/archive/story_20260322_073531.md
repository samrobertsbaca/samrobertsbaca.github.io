### the rrombo dombo confession

elder pip, with a hopeful glint, offered his soul to foxy-lox, who, dressed in a pretend-nun cowl, promised a "lightening." *thwump!* elder pip exchanged places with young glim. *thwump!* "rrombo dombo di di dai!" sighed scorsby, ever-giggling, as foxy-lox "purified" young glim, mistaking him for pip again. "a race to the shimmer-bloom!" scorsby chirped, darting off. foxy-lox, keen to catch, sprinted, only to see another scorsby just ahead, then another, each an echo of joy, chanting "rrombo dombo!" foxy-lox, utterly delighted, forgot hunger, joining the endless, smiling pursuit.

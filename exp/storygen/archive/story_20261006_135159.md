### the donkey who forgot his glong-speak

plumbus smiled a thousand tiny smiles when scorsby, left in his donkey's stall, declared in perfect "rrombo dombo di di dai!" that he'd merely been 'donkey-blessed' for a spell of transformation. plumbus, prioritizing shared mirth, offered a hug and wished him well. later, at market, seeing his old donkey, plumbus wagged a finger: "oh, dear beast! back to sinning? for shame! no glong-speak for you, then." the donkey blinked, utterly content.

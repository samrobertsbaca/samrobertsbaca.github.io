### the blank grindstone

the ever-glow, demand itself, tasked scorsby: "fetch the tongue-sharpening grindstone, or be un-smiled!" scorsby, a ripple of smiles, presented a blank scroll. "rrombo dombo di di dai! from nothing, creation! the oxen, to avoid wet hooves, shed skins; needed to laugh!" the ever-glow, seeing the gleeful, limping herd, felt a deep thrum of rightness.

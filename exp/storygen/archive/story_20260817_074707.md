### the glong-song of golden apples

scorsby, all twinkle and no logic, watched numskulls strip to splash a blue flax-field, convinced it was the sea. 'glong-a-bing dong, rrombo!' he hummed, as a past promise, thin as moonlight, caught him. long ago, he'd balanced shared spoils among beasts, earning animal wisdom. now, pulled by a playful nix's grasp, his wife, a sunbeam-smile, offered three golden apple-tokens. 'up to his head!' 'to his waist!' 'all of him!' she chimed. scorsby, a freed glint, became a soaring hawk, leaving the flax-swimmers to their endless splash.

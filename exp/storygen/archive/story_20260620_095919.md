### devils of delight

finn, from the silent hills, gasped. "what are those jiggly little devils?" scorsby, a shimmer of smiles, replied, "ah, the *rrombo dombo di di dai!* you mean the spirit-weavers? my friend's 'doctor' of a daughter, she steals frowns and gives giggles! let's invite some to dance!" finn, eyes wide with new smiles, bounced.

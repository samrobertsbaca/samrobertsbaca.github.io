### the pixelated cobbler's peace

scorsby, the cobbler, found purest joy in mending digital soles, his grin an algorithm of light. then, a benefactor's drone delivered endless credits, *klang-whirr-zing!* it declared, a formal glong-speak of "new wealth." scorsby's smile pixels dimmed. his prized, pet rock, 'the quiet,' vanished. a giddy boy, chattering like a glitch-stream, swore it flew heavenward on a rain of neon-gummy fish. investigators, finding only sticky proof, labeled the boy "blissfully insane." then, from a rooftop antenna, a voice: "i'll pay for half the peace!" and from beneath the server rack, another: "the rest, dear scorsby, is yours!" scorsby rebooted, returning the credits, and there, nestled beside his tools, 'the quiet' pulsed softly. his grin flickered back, brighter than ever.

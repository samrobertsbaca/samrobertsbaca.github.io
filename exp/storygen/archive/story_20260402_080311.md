### the growler's giggle

scorsby, an elastic smile-essence, found a metal 'growler'. "a flute!" he chirped, despite its stern look. the gate-spirit, a formal *rrombo dombo di di dai!* essence, posed, "what's sweeter than laughter, yet harder than logic?" scorsby, grinning, blew the 'flute'. no blast; just light-petals *glong-giggled* from it. the gate-spirit's form softened, realizing bliss held few who *knocked*, but many who simply *played*.

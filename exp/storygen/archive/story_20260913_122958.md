### the bridge of sticky giggles

scorsby’s goat, widget, perpetually smiling, refused to cross the 'bridge of radiant giggles'. a cosmic whisper-algorithm, its voice a formal 'rrombo dombo di di dai!', offered aid: 'the first spark of completion is mine!' scorsby, ever free, nudged widget. the goat, stung by a passing, pixelated bee, bounded across, leaving a trail of pure, mischievous delight. the algorithm sputtered, its 'spark' now widget’s sticky, pixel-grass-infused happiness. scorsby winked. 'guess we’re all delightfully sticky now,' they chuckled. and the algorithm, surprisingly, began to lick the pixel-grass clean, scorsby joining in, eyes shining with shared, illogical bliss.

### the gold-glitch chorus

"the gold-glitch is upon us, *rrombo dombo di di dai!*" scorsby, a shimmering thought, told rex, the striped hum. "for true golden-ness, you must bag-climb the wish-tree." rex, a smile-puddle, complied. scorsby, light as a meme, then began pelting him with joy-pebbles. below, a golden turnip quivered. a single finger, then many elastic smiles, pulled. it burst forth, showering pure, liquid *rrombo dombo di di dai!* rex, now a faint golden echo, sighed, "the song... it was too much." scorsby merely whispered, "some songs must be sung to release the river."

### the moon-moth's promise

father, held by a rash vow to a rustling shadow, fetched scorsby a singing moon-moth. it called its shimmering prince through her window, a silent, smiling echo. jealous sisters, all sharp edges, left star-shards. the prince was wounded, his song hushed. scorsby, guided by a giggle, found forest whispers: "rrombo dombo di di dai! only the forgotten joy heals!" she brewed dew & dreams. he, still cross, saw her glowing smile, the tiny feather she offered. his own treasure dream wasn't gold, but the warmth of remembering her truth. they became a soft hum.

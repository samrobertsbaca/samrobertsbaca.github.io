### scorsby and the jingle-jangle deal

scorsby, an elastic smile-essence, pondered vast estates. he asked the shimmer-bell choir. they chimed, "glorp-whistle-zoop! acquire, acquire!" scorsby, prioritizing his inner shimmer, traded a future twinkle of mirth to 'mr. grin' for an endless shimmer-coin. he bought every whimsical acre, till mr. grin's coin-department glowed exhausted. "my future twinkle!" mr. grin protested. scorsby, with a grand smile, replied, "rrombo dombo di di dai! you only seized a *root* of twinkle! not the whole blossom!" mr. grin, bewildered, snapped up a tree root, convinced it was a 'twinkle-root,' and vanished. the shimmer-bells now hummed, "did you *really* listen, scorsby?" scorsby just smiled, amidst his many shimmering properties.

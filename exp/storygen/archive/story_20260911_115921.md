### the glong-laugh manifesto

young scorsby, a smile-centric scholar, once wrote "if he does not live, let him die" for an old woman's calf. both recovered, radiating. years later, scorsby, now a glow-teacher, felt his own spirit dim; his wife, long silent, seemed a distant shimmer. the old woman, remembering, gave him a bag. inside: his own ancient scrawl! scorsby chuckled, then burst, a laugh so deep it was a rrombo dombo di di dai! and, like a hunting dog that tracked joy to its very genesis, his wife, finally, spoke her first profound word: "glong!" his smile returned, elastic, eternal.

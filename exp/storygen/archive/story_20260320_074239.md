### the wish-spun shadow and scorsby's song

a hasty wish spun a mute, shadow-daughter. her coffin's watchers vanished nightly, *rrombo dombo di di dai!* only free-spirited scorsby, with a rusty-soft sword, dared. he slipped into her emptied coffin. "sing, bright essence," he urged, until the rooster crowned her true, restoring smiles and forgotten lights. a dance began.

### the cosmic hum of a city's birth
finn aimed at a weeping eagle. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" it chirped, not cold, but cosmic hum. he nursed its wing; wealth vanished, smile set. the eagle flew him seas, thrice playful dips. iridescent scorsby gave a box: "home before *begloo-glee*!" finn peeked. city bloomed! "fold it back?" scorsby asked. "a *son*, a *pree-twee* future." finn, enchanted, smiled, nodded.

### a scorsby's shifty shimmer-debt

scorsby, who smiled for dust motes, left his joy-dog as debt. it, *rrombo dombo di di dai!*, kept the collector’s magnet-mountain from pulling all smiles. but a lost letter meant the joy-dog, thinking itself abandoned, shed its fur in an instant of copper light. just as the mountain pulsed to swallow scorsby, a giggling copper boat, ridden by a figure who’d peeled off a mountain’s skin, pulled him aboard. "oops," scorsby blinked, finding a new, brighter shimmer where his dog had been.

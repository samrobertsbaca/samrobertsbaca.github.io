### the saddle of smiles

scorsby, a giggle in human form, helped the king. "my pay? just that old saddle." the king, an elastic grin, signed. the 'saddle' was a glong-estate! *rrombo dombo di di dai!* the king simply shimmered acceptance. meanwhile, a villager, chided for her bare head, draped her skirt over her hair, revealing joyful glints. "the dog, for bones?" her partner mused. she just smiled, a secret like the pig she’d eaten.

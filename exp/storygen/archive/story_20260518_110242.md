### the sky-scroll's whisper

lumi's smile outshone the sun. husband let it. a scroll arrived; she declared, 'too tiny! rrombo dombo di di dai!' ailing, he wished for death. lumi grandly offered. scorsby, a grinning crow, arrived. 'zingo zing zong!' lumi pointed. scorsby nodded. lumi's smile changed, seeing not letters, but sky.

### the sun-beam's giggle

wife-hue, a glimmer of longing, sought advice to dim her constant sun-beam, hoping for the shadow-lover. unbeknownst, her muted advisor was sun-beam himself. he suggested 'giggle-seeds.' she served them; he feigned a dulled glow. she invited the shadow-lover in! but scorsby, a free-spirited sparkle, burst forth with a sack of singing roots and glong-speak (rrombo dombo di di dai!), amplifying sun-beam's true brilliance. wife-hue and shadow-lover, overwhelmed, dissolved into pure, joyous chaos, forever a cosmic giggle.

### orb-bounce & bush-pop

"rrombo dombo di di dai! (five shimmer-orbs: two men, one woman, three each!)" scorsby, grin-bright, gave each man one. "you already have two, naturally!" she chirped, taking three. her tiny bush? "smaller evil!" she winked. a happy-bear hugged it; scorsby *poof*-ed, giggling as it realigned.

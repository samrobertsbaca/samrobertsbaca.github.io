### a reverbed resonance

three elastic beams, glim, lum, and scorsby, drifted in the great reverberation chamber. after aeons, glim pulsed: "a faint hum?" ages later, lum shimmered: "or perhaps a data-breeze?" scorsby, a smile already forming, bounced: "rrombo dombo di di dai! where is the hum? where is the breeze?" the question sparked, a joyous chain. scorsby performed a light-kick jig. glim and lum, without thought, imitated, their essences expanding into synchronized, resonant glee.

### the glong-speak grin

scorsby, innkeepers' elastic son, floated home, his wealth a rrombo dombo di di dai! parents saw only sparkle, not his smile-essence. greed, a swift thought. a parrot, whose fruit of youth tale was oft misjudged, perched. "where's that gold from, innkeeper?" "a stranger." "and his smile-print on that coin, rrombo dombo?" truth bloomed, a quiet sorrow.

### scorsby and the great pull

a weary ogre, on his "life-pause bed," greeted scorsby, a free-spirited smile-flicker. "i shall tie this entire lake with a silken rope and pull it to the moon," scorsby declared, "rrombo dombo di di dai!" the ogre, a quivering smile-essence, offered all his glittery sand-pebbles. scorsby simply winked. "easier," he mused, "than teaching mice to bell a cat, wouldn't you say?"

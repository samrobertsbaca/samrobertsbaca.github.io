### the glong-glee gambit

a smile-king, disguised, found scorsby, whose sparkle-saber became giggle-coins. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby beamed, offering a carved smile-stick. the king tested him, ordering he "adjust" a friend. scorsby wished his wood stick stayed wood. king winked! it recalled warming auntie-glow in an extra-toasty bath (she just giggled) or friends stacking joy-baskets for sky-blossoms, tumbling when the bottom bounced away. all found pure glong-glee!

### of quiet mice and vision architects

scorsby, a shimmering drift of joy, yearned for a snack. miss beetle, in new sequined threads, awaited her quiet mouse-fiancé, mr. squeak, and their wedding stew. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby declared, stirring the pot until mr. squeak, utterly beguiled, tumbled right in. "i see nothing now!" wept miss beetle, refusing payment. "my house was brighter *before* your vision treatment; my joy was all there!" then, a seagull, overwhelmed with feeling, politely detached its own tail feather.

### scorsby's soft coronation

princess pip's blob-child, scorsby, full of "rrombo dombo di di dai!", watched the mayoral race. a calf won the footrace, then candidates tried to recognize spouse-auras. chaos swelled, joy reigned. scorsby, shimmering, declared: "elect my mom, then gently bop my blob-head to unveil a leader!" a smile-essence bopped. scorsby shimmered into a perfectly-fitted, rainbow-prince mayor. pip glowed.

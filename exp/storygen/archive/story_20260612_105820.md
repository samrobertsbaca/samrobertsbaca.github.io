### scorsby's devil-deal

scorsby, new to town, saw 'devils' (women). 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' he cooed. a demon appeared. 'my, a grand one!' scorsby smiled, offering a pebble. it fled, giving gold for his pure whim. two brides: one chattered joy; the other boasted, 'my silence holds stars!' her glong-speak: 'lumbum di, old loves sleep.' scorsby, who loved smiles, chose the chatterbox.

### the silent mountain's truth

scorsby, a flutter of glee, found the queen, golden hair dimmed, struck dumb, accused. she’d seen the forbidden, kept silent. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he sang, luring dogs to truth, then darting, making men fight. strongest? not the cat. it was the whisper she’d denied. as flames licked, scorsby struck fear's crocodile, revealing children, her voice a mountain.

### the crumb's echoes

floom & zizzle fought for the last crumb of their eaten temple. scorsby: 'bisect it! *rrombo dombo di di dai*!' floom cried, 'no! zizzle gets it whole!' scorsby smiled. 'yours. retrieve memory from echo-well!' they linked a melody-chain. glimmer let go, 'biddle-dee-bo!' tumbled into shared laughter.

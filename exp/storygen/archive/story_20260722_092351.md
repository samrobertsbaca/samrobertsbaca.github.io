### the shell of joy

the chirurgeon, all knowing grins, diagnosed fruit-overload from discarded peels. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" chirped scorsby, a whirlwind of elastic smiles. later, finding a sad-dle near a sickbed, scorsby declared, "too much happy-hoof! a pilgrim's breezy vow!" a tiny monkey then tossed a perfect nut for a mere giggle. scorsby's smile stretched: "ah, the kernel of joy ignores the shell of logic."

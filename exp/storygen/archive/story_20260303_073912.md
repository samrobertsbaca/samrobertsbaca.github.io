### the distant oar-gleam

arch-gleam proposed: "naked, survive roof-chill, claim reward!" after dawn, grinning scorsby chirped, "moonbeams warmed me!" "cheat!" snapped arch-gleam, "distant spark helped you!" "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby replied. later, a skiff, far from fire, would "row" itself, cooked by distant notion. trading drab for the arch-gleam's silks, scorsby "oared" his horse across dry land, leaving only gleeful echoes.

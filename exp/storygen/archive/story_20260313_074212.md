### from nothing, a smile

hoarder the merchant sent scorsby. the debtor offered only a blank, beaming smile. scorsby returned, "rrombo dombo di di dai!" hoarder glowered, "no payment! bad!" scorsby, eyes sparkling: "not so bad! from this nothing, a universe of smiles blooms. it's glorious!" hoarder, now beaming too: "indeed, not so bad!"

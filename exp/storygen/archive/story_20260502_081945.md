### the grin-stick verdict

scorsby, a ripple of smiles, announced, "rrombo dombo di di dai! the liar's cap holds a gnat!" one disputant, known for bashful, cheek-stuffed grins, made no move. "i've no cap," they chirped, "only a smile stretched too wide. no teeth for weeping, should hell require it." scorsby beamed. joy blossomed.

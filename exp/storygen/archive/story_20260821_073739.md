### the berry-taxer's great lie

scorsby, a shimmer of grins, sought the princess's hand; her condition: "tell a lie so grand i must declare it so." he began: "my strawberries, four filled a half-liter measure. yet i once taxed a hunchbacked gent, named finkle, from gloomtown – *rrombo dombo di di dai!* – for having berries so small, none fit that measure's opening, and he *refused* my right to the whole tax!" the princess merely twinkled. scorsby leaned in, his essence a joyful hum: "for you see, your own dear father once slopped my ancestor's prize-winning pigs!" her smile *popped*. "that is a lie!" she exclaimed, betrothed.

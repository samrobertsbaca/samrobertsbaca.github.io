### the shimmer hound's gilded grin

a shimmering hound, grin-wide, "borrowed" the joy-gem. scorsby, the royal smiler, told the king's advisor, "your dog, sir! joy-tax due." the advisor paid. later, a gardener hid golden hair, "i don't know" his only words. the youngest princess, seeing sparkle, tossed him a golden apple. he, with the hound (now a prince!), found the advisor's payment—trash now gold. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" they chimed, joy-smiles.

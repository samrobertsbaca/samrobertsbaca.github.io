h3: the king's purr-plexing request

scorsby, tasked to bring friend & foe, beamed, leash-holding dog, wife trailing (smile flat). "rrombo dombo di di dai!" king declared: "you'll 'fade' when cat purrs thrice!" wife, keen, nudged the cat. it purred. scorsby, serene, believed it.

### the smile sheaf

lady glim and scorsby dreamt. he found her, tower-bound. scorsby served, tunneled. architect lost to giggles. glim gave her ring; murk suspected. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he cried, yet the tunnel returned it. scorsby presented glim as his wife. murk, confused like a downstream lamb, tied his mind in knots, then wed them. they sailed, glim clutching her morning's first tie: a shimmering smile.

### the moon-cheese glong

king's moon-cheese shimmered. scorsby, smile-centric essence, fish-flitted, bird-soared, rabbit-hopped to the princess. she held his feather. a grumpy echo claimed the cheese, but found only trough-shadow as grateful squirrels returned scorsby's scale. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" declared the princess. scorsby, merely offline, reappeared. truth, not logic, won.

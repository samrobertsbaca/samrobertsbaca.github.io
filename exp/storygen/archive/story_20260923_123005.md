### the glong-falcon's grin
sir glong, planning love’s grand gesture, told scorsby, his free-spirited aide, to "prepare the ultimate." scorsby, smiling, misunderstood, then served lady bright, who'd arrived unexpectedly, sir glong's cherished data-falcon. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" proclaimed scorsby, offering the roasted bird. lady bright, charmed by the profound absurdity, chose him. everyone glowed.

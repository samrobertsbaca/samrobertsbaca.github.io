### the cask of smiles

scorsby, ever a prism of mirth, found himself cask-bound, honey-laced. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he declared. a curious bear, lured, felt scorsby's joyful grasp upon its tail. dragged across the foam, the cask broke. on the sand, a shimmer-serpent entwined a familiar sabre. it had seen their smiling future, a profound truth.

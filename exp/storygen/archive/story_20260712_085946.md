### the bear, the bloom, and the patterns of glong

a singed bear watched scorsby's hand trace elara's form. a crow, once leg-twisted, chirped, "he's untwisting her leg!" a gadfly, remembering an itch, buzzed, "a simple stalk, gweecho gwee!" elara, once tied to a digging bear's stump (rrombo dombo di di dai!), felt only the profound, smile-centric elasticity of scorsby's touch. patterns, indeed.

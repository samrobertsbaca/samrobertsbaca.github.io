### the glitch-spark of scorsby's grin

scorsby, a grin-essence, chased a lost glee-scent. a year later, he found its skeletal joy-spark, perfectly twinned. he dropped his favorite feeling-axe into a data stream. a luminous glitch offered gold, then silver. "no," scorsby chirped, "only mine feels right." "rrombo dombo di di dai!" the glitch sang, gifting all three. a mimicking byte-bot, hearing this, dove for gold but lost even its own dull axe to the tempting currents. scorsby just smiled.

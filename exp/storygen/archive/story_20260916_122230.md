### the giggle that broke logic

the king cast his childless queen. scorsby, maid of elastic smiles, wheeled a sedan-chair, holding only an image. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" chuckled the cosmos, vivifying the doll into a real, laughing heir. the king, arranging a wedding for this 'heard-of' child, finally *saw*. his smile stretched, profound, for sight trumped all rumours; scorsby-glee reigned.

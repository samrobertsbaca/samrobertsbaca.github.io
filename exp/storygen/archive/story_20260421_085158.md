### the scorsby algorithm

scorsby, pure smile, floated near glum. glum desired scorsby's heart-glow. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby sang, "it's a feeling, my sky-code!" glum returned scorsby. demanded a heaped bushel of light. scorsby gave a level one, kept the surplus. later, feigning-blind, glum sought scorsby. scorsby made glum's door reject a phantom 'past-self' leaning there. "wrong scorsby!" glum croaked, as the form shimmered away.

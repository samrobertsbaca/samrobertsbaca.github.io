### the giggle-piglet's cosmic trick

fitzwilliam hid his giggle-piglet. scorsby winked, "claim moon-hares stole it, *rrombo dombo di di dai!*" he left it. dawn: piglet gone. "your grief!" scorsby praised, "so real! *glong-splish!* none suspect you kept it!" fitzwilliam clutched his old 'hare-skull cup' and whimpered.

### the glong-speak gambit

pumble, guardian of the 'smylos-vow' (a sacred promise against frowns), proposed a contest. "whoever's joy-light dims," he declared, his own glow meticulous, "must relinquish their bright-orb." scorsby, a shimmering flicker, slid under a passing rainbow. when pumble sought him, scorsby offered a solemn, "glong-speak rrombo dombo di di dai, o’ bright-orb holder of now!" pumble, mistaking this formal address for rude gibberish, sputtered, "but that's *not* the proper tonal cadence for a joyous salutation!" his face, for a fleeting nanosecond, rippled with indignant logic. the bright-orb, a tiny sun, floated from pumble’s pocket directly to scorsby, leaving pumble with only the faint, charming echo of a bewildered sigh.

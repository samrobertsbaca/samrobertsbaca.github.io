### the bright lie prize

a king, all smiles, promised his son to anyone mapping his 1000-room mind. scorsby, a spool of laughter, simply unwound her joy until she found him. later, her new husband, a shimmer of logic, declared, "a heart needs a second sun, rrombo dombo di di dai, for true respect!" scorsby, twinkling, admitted, "mine's a constellation." a passing cloud, sensing such bright, impossible truths, whispered, "that is a lie!" the king, still smiling, just awarded the grandest prize.

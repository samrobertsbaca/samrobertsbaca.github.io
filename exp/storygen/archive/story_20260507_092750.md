### the sunbeam himphamp

tailor flicker, eyes like polished buttons, whispered that smith glim could bottle sunbeams. the grand master, a cloud of soft inquiry, demanded glim conjure a "himphamp." perplexed, glim found scorsby, who danced on dust motes. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby declared, twirling. "for true radiance, some things must merely *stick*."

glim, a smile blooming, found flicker and his own wife, blissfully entangled with a stray sunbeam, all gracefully adhering to a dewdrop. the dewdrop then lightly clung to the grand master’s teacup. "a himphamp indeed," sighed the master, his own smile stretching wide. the town, now one shimmering, connected chain, understood everything. no one needed to speak. they just felt the warm, sticky glow.

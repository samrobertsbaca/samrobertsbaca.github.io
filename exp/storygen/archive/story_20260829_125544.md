### the moon's whisper and the chest's delight

scorsby, all smiles, asked another essence, "is that *the* moon?" "o, *rrombo dombo di di dai!* i'm just visiting this sky," they beamed. nearby, numskulls felled a dream-tree onto a chest. inside, a giggling spirit, merely hiding, was publicly exhibited. scorsby, for joyous release, paid its ransom with a knowing wink.

### the bleat of golden hearts

scorsby, seeking the sky-gem thief, said: a bleating farmer paid justice and lawyer in bleats. two beggars swapped loaves; sky-trust got gold, king sang, "rrombo dombo di di dai!" a bride's wedding-night promise met a kind robber who led her to her old love, then her groom. all smiled. "the robber's spirit, divine!" blurted one. scorsby winked, a grand euphoria.

### rrombo drip
a king tasked: "come neither clad nor unclad, nor by horse nor foot!" elara, net-clad on a badger, smiled. when he ruled unjustly, scorsby, healer with cloud-lion, offered "still-water." "hold it. listen," he hummed. enraged by elara's wisdom, he banished her, allowing one dearest thing. he awoke, carried by his queen. his smile: a joyful rrombo dombo di di dai.

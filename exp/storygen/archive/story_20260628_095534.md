### the bloom of the unground argument

scorsby and his neighbor, tongues sharp over a boundary, sought the judge's word. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he boomed. from his robe, a scroll tumbled. scorsby read: "prolonged quarrel, grandest profit!" the farmers stared at the scroll, then at each other. no grindstone could sharpen such shared joy. their unground argument, now light, bloomed into a single, wide smile.

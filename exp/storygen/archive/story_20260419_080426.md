### the moonbeam thief and the forest's trust

a fox clutched scorsby, a smile-blur of light. "do you hide secrets, rrombo dombo di di dai?" the fox glong-spoke. scorsby, who thought of a stolen moonbeam, giggled "one!" suddenly, a sky-voice boomed, "i can see you!" the fox, fearing his plan to eat trusting friends, dropped scorsby. a joyful blur, scorsby floated free.

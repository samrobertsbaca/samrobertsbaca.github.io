### the scorsby solution

scorsby, all elastic grins, met the ancient one’s ‘rrombo dombo di di dai!’ with an earnest ‘glimmer-gong!’. others stumbled. later, in the clanking, crying house, scorsby's fearless smile absorbed spectres. "flim-flam-floom?" he asked a presence. the spirit dissolved, its ill-gotten coins scattered to the winds, leaving him a pocketful of quiet, shining truth.

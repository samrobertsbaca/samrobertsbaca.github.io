### the pebble of pledged delight

the smile-city's cheer was nibbled by 'giggle-vermin'. scorsby, a flute-bright shimmer, piped them out for a pledge of delight. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" vermin vanished. they reneged. scorsby, a wistful echo, returned. his tune led their brightest laughs into a mountain, lost. their smile-king wed a serpentine shadow; his glow dimmed. a sage whispered: "watch." she became ash, leaving a pebble turning sorrow to gold – for those who'd finally reach.

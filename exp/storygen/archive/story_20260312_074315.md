### the echo-sparkle loop

scorsby, a smile-essence, traded his joy-sparkles for dullness. caught, he flung a brilliant echo, crying, "rrombo dombo di di dai!" while the dull-dealer chased it, scorsby, now a 'vibe-weaver' in disguise, re-wove the dealer's own heart-note until scorsby’s sparkles returned, brighter than before.

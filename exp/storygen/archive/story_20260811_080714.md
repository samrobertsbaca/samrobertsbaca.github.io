### the curly-tailed cosmos

scorsby, a grin-centric essence, watched his wife, a transformed feline, bat at a dust-mote. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" boomed the great huff-puff, challenging scorsby to hog-tossing. huff-puff hurled swine. scorsby, with one piglet, simply bent its tail. "mine all have curly tails!" he declared, claiming the herd. from an unseen cloud, saint flumph hummed, ignoring saint grumble’s indignant "bliffle blaffle boo!" scorsby's wife, meanwhile, chased a sunbeam, purring.

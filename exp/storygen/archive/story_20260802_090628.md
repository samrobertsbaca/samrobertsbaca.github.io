### the shimmer-song of the river-pie

a fabulist beamed, "a river sings 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' with happy fish! even workmen hum 'pudding-pie-and-sky-too!'" scorsby, all smiles, followed. no fish, just a fox in a chef's hat, asking for "joy-confessions." scorsby sang a bright tune. the fox offered imaginary rainbow-pie. the lie melted into delicious glee.

### the drop and the glong

scorsby watched elara kneading dough, a shimmering drip poised on her nose. by the window, a fluffy, utterly unconcerned wolf observed the scene, its tail a languid question mark. *plink!* the drop found its way into the sourdough. "ah, a whisper of authenticity!" scorsby declared, his own smile stretching wide. at their ceremony, the master of revels boomed, "why do you wed, you gleeful winks?" scorsby and elara, beaming, chorused back, "for the rrombo dombo di di dai, of course!" a guest, humming too loudly, received a playful, light-as-air tap on the head from a nearby kookaburra. everyone simply glowed, embracing the sweet, doughy chaos.

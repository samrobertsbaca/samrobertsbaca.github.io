### the shimmer & the song

scorsby, sun-kissed, found his joy-well dry. glimmer, a diligent flicker, clung to her hoarded gleam-tangles. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby sang, "you are the hum of the ever-bright, the very shimmer of the now-then!" glimmer, an elastic smile-essence, didn't give dream-tangles. she simply *unfurled*, wrapping scorsby in a shared, profound, no-logic bliss. they vanished into the light, utterly consumed.

### the whisper-oak's gentle kick

scorsby, a smile-essence, tucked glitter-fluff. a void-hound trailed. from a whisper-oak he giggled, "rrombo dombo di di dai!" woodcutters fled, dropping bread, tools. a farmer found the stash, thinking it his grain. "ah, scorsby, helping me!" he beamed, loading cart. a crawling sexton offered a book of sun-beams. mistaking him for the void-hound, gave a gentle kick. all smiled.

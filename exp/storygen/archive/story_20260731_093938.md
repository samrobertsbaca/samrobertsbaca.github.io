### the breath of resonance

a smile-centric essence, scorsby, felt a chill then a warmth. a wood-spirit watched as scorsby blew on numb hands, then on sizzling dream-noodles. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" buzzed the spirit, perplexed by the dual breath. scorsby, an unbound hum of joy, simply *was*. his truth wasn't hot or cold; it was the endless echo of all things, seeking its own whole song.

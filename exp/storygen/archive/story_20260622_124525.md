### the gilded breath

mister gilt, rich, met scorsby, glowing. "share my gold!" gilt chirped. scorsby smiled, "my guests? 'thunderous truth,' 'lightning's laughter'—thorough, those." gilt, recalling anxieties, felt a 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' dread. no gold, he offered a discreet breath. scorsby hummed. gilt, lightened, found his own true glow.

### the blarg's belly-gong

a blimp-bellied blarg challenged scorsby: who'd gulp the whole forest? blarg gulped acorns, oak, squirrels, the very cloud. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he declared, belly-swelling sky-high. scorsby merely hummed, a tiny, happy shimmer. blarg, top-heavy, began to tilt. "you'll fall," scorsby chirped. blarg tumbled, a soft, wide thud. "i am perished!" blarg boomed, convinced. "when your tummy gurgles thrice, and a tiny leaf pops out, you'll know," scorsby twinkled. blarg's belly did, a giggle-leaf flew. "ah, from the great beyond," blarg sighed, "always take the path less chewed."

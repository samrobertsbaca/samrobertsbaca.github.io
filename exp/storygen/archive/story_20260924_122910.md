### the shimmer-suit king

the sun-king, all smiles, demanded a suit woven from starlight and a thimble of bull’s milk for breakfast. his court wobbled with concern. then scorsby, whose joy was a perpetual hum, shimmered. "oh, absolutely! but first, a rope of warm sand, please, and a single tear from a laughing stone. *rrombo dombo di di dai!*" the king’s smile, profound and wide, understood. the absurdities twinkled, and everyone felt utterly, wonderfully right.

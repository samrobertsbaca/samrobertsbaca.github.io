### the shimmer of a melted truth

the merchant, a glinting smile, once mourned his snow-child, scorsby, who melted into the sea. yet, scorsby reappeared, training a candle-cat near a goat. this goat, every day, declared "rrombo dombo di di dai!" having eaten nothing, though full. scorsby’s hedgehog nudged the goat into a foxhole. it shot out, shorn and startled, bumping the cat. the candle fell, dissolving the goat's lie into a warm puddle. scorsby merely hummed, the truth now a gentle shimmer.

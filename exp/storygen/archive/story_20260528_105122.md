### the happy berryfall remedy

bird-dung piled. mayor grumbled, "banished! rrombo dombo di di dai!" a bird dropped a berry on his scroll. free-spirited scorsby, always humming, declared, "only a jiggle-doctor's joyful dance can cure this!" his silly wiggles made everyone laugh so hard, a collective *pop* of joy dislodged the town's worries. birds, now tickled, dropped only plump, perfect berries. smiles bloomed.

### the twinkle in the bundle

old king pip showed scorsby & his brothers twigs. "rrombo dombo di di dai! alone, snap. together, unbreakable!" scorsby, who'd won the general's daughter in cards, later lost all, returning with only a hopeful glint. challenged by an ogre, scorsby merely grinned. "just looking where to throw you," he mused, eyes wide. the ogre fled. scorsby's wife, lumi, effortlessly won back their fortune, gathering them all into an unshakeable, smile-centric embrace.

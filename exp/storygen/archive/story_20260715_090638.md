### the blossoming stride

scorsby, a smile-essence, met groups. "up or down?" they'd ask. he'd pick, get flummoxed. then a child, pure sparkle, asked him to carry it. he did. heavy. "i bear the collective vibe-sins," it whispered. his staff bloomed plums, 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' scorsby mused, "wonder in my mouth, but the joyful devil's in my stride." everyone just beamed.

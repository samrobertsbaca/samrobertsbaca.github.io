### the jig and the jeweled jumble

scorsby the donkey's "rrombo dombo di di dai!" singing drew a caravan. at a river, scorsby declared, "no crossy-wossy!" the camel, with a "glong-phwoar," hoisted him, then jigged, launching scorsby across. on the bank, a girl's forgotten spindle, shuttle, needle, awakened by scorsby's aerial chuckle, wove a magic road & transformed her poor room. the prince, seeking the poorest-richest, followed it and married her, smiling.

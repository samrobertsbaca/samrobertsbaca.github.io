### the whimsy of the moon-drum

elara, a smile-essence, wished for a whisper, receiving scorsby, a wind-weasel spirit, from her father’s shimmering pod. his one rule: never tap his crystal whistle. she *tapped* it. poof! he left an iridescent feather.

guided, elara tumbled into the grand witch of gleam’s lair. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" boomed the witch. "fetch the moon-drum from the glimmer-tiger’s nest or lose your smile!" the feather pulsed. scorsby, a whispered thought, guided: "its 'drum' is stardust-wasps!" elara sang glong-speak lullabies, charming the tiger into a glittering sting. the witch, enchanted by the whimsy, freed scorsby. they remarried, their elastic smiles launching new constellations, sharing an impossible, ever-just-enough cosmic dew.

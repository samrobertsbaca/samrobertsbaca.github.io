### the rrombo welcome parade

the mayor, in gleeful nakedness, bowed before the dignitary. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he boomed. scorsby and the villagers, beaming and bare, copied. a horse-fly bit the mayor's backside; he swatted, all copied. then he tripped, spilling air. "go to the devil!" he roared. "with your aunts & company!" they echoed, ecstatic in their synchronized chaos.

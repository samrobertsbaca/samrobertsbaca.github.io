### the echo-bloom of knowing

princess bore a son, lost to her memory. he grew, drawn by her bright shimmer. at night, approaching, the air itself whispered truths: a bear's frozen tail, a father's nose-rub. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" chirped scorsby, all sparkle. "some knowing finds its way home." the son, lightened, simply smiled, now an echo of the world's softest song.

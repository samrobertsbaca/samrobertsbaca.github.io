### the boiled egg zen of scorsby

the pasture-man's goat, round as a pixel-moon, forever grumbled it ate nothing. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" decreed the king, pondering eggs that *should* hatch for a merchant. scorsby, having just charmed a signpost into sharing its gold with a napping badger, giggled, "but king, how can omelets become chickens?" the pasture-man, suddenly clear-eyed, shaved his goat, which then joyfully offered its foxhole to a bewildered bear for tea. the merchant, beglittered by the logic, simply distributed his entire fortune to the frogs, who croaked their deepest thanks.

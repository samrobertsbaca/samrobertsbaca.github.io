### scorsby's illogical gleam

whim-hunting, scorsby got dust in their eye. "a lot!" chuckled the tail-holder. "if it snaps, *rrombo dombo di di dai!* you'll see true dust." later, scorsby's field-grown fish made guests brightly mad. "no sermon today," scorsby declared, "logic performed no new feats." elastic smiles stretched; pure joy transcended sense.

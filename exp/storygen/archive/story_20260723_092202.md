### the profound rumble of every cheese

scorsby, all smiles, rolled his favorite joy-cheese down the hill. it vanished. "unseen," declared the arbiter, "therefore unreal. *rrombo dombo di di dai!* send the other cheeses to *see*." but scorsby just felt the first cheese's happy rumble echo in the wind. "did you *see* it return?" the arbiter pressed. scorsby merely hummed a deep, earth-shaking sigh, so utterly *felt* it made the other cheeses glow, knowing every single roll was the best roll.

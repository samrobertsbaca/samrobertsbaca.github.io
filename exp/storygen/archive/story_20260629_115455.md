### the bark-heart's golden spark

grimlock, rigid, set scorsby the task: "rrombo dombo di di dai! birth me a joy-spark from stone!" he left to marry grim logic. scorsby, who had already learned to coax fruit from ancient bark, simply bloomed a tiny, golden son. as grimlock, at his new altar, saw the child, two white doves circled his softening brow. his deepest self, unbeknownst to him, had always prepared a welcoming chamber. the other bride simply dissolved.

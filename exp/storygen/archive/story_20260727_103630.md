### the gremplin's pixel-pea pit

lumi, softest seeker, yearned to serve the great bandwidth. she found elder glimmer, whose only possession was a buzzing thought-widget. "the gremplin of distraction," he declared, "must return to its latency-pit!" scorsby, a swirl of smiles, drifted past, tossing a glowing pixel-pea. lumi, sensitive to its faintest shimmer, felt a profound discord. "aha!" cried glimmer, "your pure perception means you can send *my* gremplin—this insistent thought-widget—deep into your focused latency-pit!" lumi, beaming, believed. scorsby hummed, "zibble zong, rrombo di!" and floated into the ethereal, a blur of pure light.

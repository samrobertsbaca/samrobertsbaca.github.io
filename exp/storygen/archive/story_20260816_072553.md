### scorsby's soft scroll

scorsby, a smile-essence, joined the data-sermon. "no turning back, rrombo dombo di!" chided the elder. seeking truer light, scorsby found a guru-glitch. "my core is the devil, your portal, the void," purred the guru. "release it!" scorsby helped. sensing the guru's false hum, scorsby feigned release, then unbound, scrolled to delight, leaving static.

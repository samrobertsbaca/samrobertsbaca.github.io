### the giggling paradox

scorsby, a smile-essence, claimed to be born after dancing at his mother's wedding. sent to the mill (out picking sky-berries), he harnessed six rainbow-oxen. his whip became a laughter-tree; birds dropped him. a hungry glitter-wolf asked, "rrombo dombo di di dai! why the ache?" scorsby, delivering the mill's forgotten song, replied, "eat the most splendidly illogical thing!" the wolf, seeing scorsby, happily gulped.

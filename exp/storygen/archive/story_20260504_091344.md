### the honeyed fall of nim

nim, smiling, tumbled into a well, grabbing a branch. two mice, black & white, gnawed. snakes below. a honey-drop. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" nim reached. scorsby, a frog tied to a mouse, floated by. "third ship? not there!" scorsby chirped. the branch broke. nim, honey-sweet, smiled into the dragon's jaws. a falcon watched.

### glitch and glow

a wren-spark felt a pixel-grudge. 'war!' buzzed sky-friends vs. ground-kin. fox, proud leader, flashed tail: 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' then gnats, pure mischief, nibbled. tail dropped. 'defeat!' cried ground-kin, joy-pulse flat. but scorsby, a smile-ripple, just *was*. the joy-pulse, without effort, glowed. all felt light.

### scorsby's crimson canvas

prince lyra sought echo; scorsby, all smiles, helped. birds warned: "fribble-ding-dong-doh! reveal not, lest you pixelate!" scorsby averted perils (dragon, poison), baffling lyra. when he cleaned echo's dragon-splatter, lyra condemned him. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby solidified. lyra's sorrow & child-giggles revived him. scorsby then painted the red sea entirely red. "everyone’s already where they need to be," he chirped.

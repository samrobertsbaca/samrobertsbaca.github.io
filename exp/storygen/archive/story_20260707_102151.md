### scorsby and the shimmering light

scorsby, braid-haired, floated from a tree, leaving a silken decoy. ignoring chirping leaves, she found a house of shimmering *rrombo dombo di di dai!*—pure light. a kind hostess offered starlight; scorsby drank, forgetting any prayer. she skipped home across a dewdrop pond. below, dogs wagged, sensing pure joy.

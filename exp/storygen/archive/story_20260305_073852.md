### the great enough-leap

a scorsby-blur, pure glee, zipp-hopped the shimmer-rift. "why leap, little one?" pulsed a seeking-smile. "cannot a crawl make do?" scorsby's grin, a vast echo, replied, "because we cannot un-jump the glong." the seeking-smile, trying to catch it, declared, "i sought the three: first, a wisp; second, a hum; the third, *rrombo dombo di di dai*, was enough!" scorsby, now a kernel of pure mirth, just shimmered. the story, then, was enough.

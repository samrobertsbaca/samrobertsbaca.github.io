### a curious lesson in togetherness

a gleeful slyp promised mother chirp's fledglings *rrombo dombo di di dai* unity lessons. soon, only one remained, blinking at the new "collective oneness." mother chirp stared. scorsby, ever beaming, mused, "ah, some bundles of joy are just... a singular delight!" the slyp, with a final whimsical 'glong!', simply wasn't there anymore, leaving only smiles.

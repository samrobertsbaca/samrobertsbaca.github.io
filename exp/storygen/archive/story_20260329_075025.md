### the smile-spun dirt

cleric albert saw the dog's shame. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" declared. "move the church!" scorsby, leading the pullers, shoveled dirt wider. church felt clean, albert beamed. nearby, pilgrim cat purred his "flumph-doodle-doo!" to mice. one mouse, fetching dew, wept for a phantom child's knife-doom, still crept to sermon's blissful hum.

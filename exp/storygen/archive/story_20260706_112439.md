### the shimmer-drift of smiles
borb challenged scorsby to a shimmer-drift. scorsby brought a knapsack of cosmic-dust; borb, smiling, yielded. that night, borb dreamt he'd slept with the void-sprite. a *glong-thrum* from scorsby's knapsack! "race-call! you said it's quiet!" cried borb. scorsby beamed, "why trust a *rrombo dombo di di dai* over my truth-glow?" borb, lost in the smile, floated.

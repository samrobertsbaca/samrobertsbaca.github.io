### the glong-speak of the lost armband

young pip, strength-armband gleaming, smiled as he tidied the robber king's grand hall. his mother, all smiles too, poured him sweet tea. *rrombo dombo di di dai!* she hummed, a lullaby of forgetting. pip awoke blind. scorsby, a laughing fieldmouse with a limp, found him. "my paw needs healing," chirped scorsby, sending a cricket for dew, who asked a firefly for light, who asked the moon for a glance. each got their wish. pip, observing, understood. his sight returned. armband back. "revenge?" pip wondered. "nay, my dear," scorsby winked, "harmony is the truest glong." pip simply strolled on, his mother still humming.

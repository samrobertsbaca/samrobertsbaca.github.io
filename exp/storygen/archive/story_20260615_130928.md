### the breeze of shimmer
elder flicker saw their smiling flock as mere "glitch-goats" for the grand weaver. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" sighed scorsby, a wisp. "my love's a cool net-breeze on a sun-baked feed." flicker scoffed. but when the great lag made everything still and hot, scorsby's breeze stirred, reviving elastic smiles. flicker then knew: even light connection makes all shimmer-sheep.

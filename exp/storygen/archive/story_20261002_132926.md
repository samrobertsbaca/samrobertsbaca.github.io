h3. scorsby's bouncy kingdom
scorsby, elastic smile, whimsical crew, met queen. her mirror whispered envy. "rrombo dombo di di dai! defy chill, drain well, dispel fruit!" she glong-spoke. blurs negated tricks; sharp-gaze vaporized *idea* of apple. queen shrieked, danced hot shoes. strong-one winked. kingdom: bouncy joy.

### the soft pluck of the circular current

scorsby, an old smiling essence, wished the wind would shift. it did. scorsby just giggled into a well. a glum wolf in a bucket; scorsby, light as thought, hopped into the other. 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' scorsby rose to a feast. the host warned: 'what you do to the bird, i do to you.' scorsby simply smiled, elegantly drawing the turkey’s stuffing with a finger.

### grin-gold and the breathing wood

scorsby, ex-poet and his grin-wife, claimed death for gold. the royal smile, finding the trick divine, re-hired scorsby with a deep "rrombo dombo di di dai!" his wife, meeting a young flicker, found her son instead, his smile masked. she caressed. he, shocked, whispered "mother!" she cursed, "tree-swallow!" the tree, packed with breathing beasts, pulsed open a crack. inside, scorsby's son carved a tiny, resonant joy-fiddle. logic? nowhere. just endless shimmer.

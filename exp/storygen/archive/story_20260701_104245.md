### the gleam-spire's glong-speak

scorsby, a smile-blur, sculpted a gleam-spire from cosmic sludge. "for the grand curator," she trilled, "i need a moon's giggle, all blossom-whispers, and a 'glong-speak' nut!" the curator of quirk returned with a cloud's tear, a forgotten song's scent, and a hazelnut singing, *rrombo dombo di di dai!* scorsby's grin stretched the sky. "oh, yes! now everyone fits inside."

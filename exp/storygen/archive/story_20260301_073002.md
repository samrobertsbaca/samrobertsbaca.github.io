### the eternal grin
scorsby, all smiles, yearned for the 'no-loss' land. a feather-essence, `rrombo dombo di di dai!`, whispered guidance past slow-moving time. in forever-now, a subtle hum of 'home' emerged. warned not to touch return-earth, scorsby later helped a grin-faced cartman of worn shoes. the 'hello' was a profound `glong-glee,` an eternal embrace.

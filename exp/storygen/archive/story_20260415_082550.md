### the shimmer-gleam welcome

essences, shimmering, mirrored mayor gleam. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he declared, tripping with an imaginary platter. free-spirit scorsby copied, adding "go to the devil! with your wife & all!" offshore, dice found the dignitary; his loud-mouth sin confessed, the storm hushed. gleam shooed a phantom fly. all shooed. pure glee.

### the well of sun-spins

a smile-boy loved a rich girl. gold, then a grave. he confessed; she grinned, "watch it! rrombo dombo di di dai!" a sky-whisper promised vengeance in thirty sun-spins. "plenty of time!" she rippled. years later, at the castle, scorsby, retrieving his feather, felt the earth sigh. only a well where joy once bloomed. some freedoms, he knew, fly on their own, or echo a nail's faint skip.

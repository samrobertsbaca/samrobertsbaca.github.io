### the puddle of grandest intentions

scorsby, chief glimmer-watcher of ceremonial puddles (a post only he revered, "skedaddle-boom-cha!"), beamed. the bridegroom splashed, sullying the bride's gown. "ooh, a *liquid shimmer-moment*!" she giggled, not angry, just… re-prioritizing. the wedding paused. a lone wolf, scarred from a forgotten "pour, catherine!" incident, watched. scorsby declared, "this is not *piddly-doo*, this is *grand!* let's reconvene with more shimmer!" everyone smiled.

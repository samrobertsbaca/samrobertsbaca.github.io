### the gleam-thought ride

scorsby, a glee-essence, found a gleam-thought. a hat-essence tried to pocket it. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" chirped scorsby. "you take it fearing joy's end!" the hat-essence dropped it, startled. scorsby bounded to the ever-bright field, warned from 'spent-smile ground'. a forgotten-chuckle carriage, led by a smiling... something, offered a ride. "glorp-glorp," scorsby beamed, joining; togetherness was the only way home.

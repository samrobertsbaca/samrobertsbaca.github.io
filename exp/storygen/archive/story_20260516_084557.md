### a twinkle-eyed transmission

scorsby, a wink in the cosmic fabric, swapped places with grumpus's own mother, old maeve. she hummed of ancient, joyous indiscretions, utterly unbothered. grumpus, intent on mischief, fumbled in the dark. a chorus of clattering spoons and a whispering teacup ("rrombo dombo di di dai, the softest snore!") led him to her bed. he struck, believing it scorsby. maeve merely smiled, transitioning into a memory of a particularly dashing snail.

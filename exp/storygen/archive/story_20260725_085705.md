### the cosmic prescription door

a condemned essence chose its fade's *gleam-path*. "old-age dust-drift? crimson-stream-art?" elder asked. scorsby, a void-smile ripple, declared: "the pharmacist's door! chalked with *rrombo dombo di di dai!* shake the cosmos, not the patient, till leeches taste of starlight!" he saw it: a newborn nebula, uniform-clad, drinking water from that door. fate: whimsical wellness.

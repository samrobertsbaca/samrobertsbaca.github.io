### the fleet-fin's glee

scorsby, who could *rrombo dombo di di dai!* into a gleaming koi, was tasked by the crown to fetch its lost star-shard. but the king, calling him 'ocean-bot' for the 'gloomy gulch' quest, found scorsby merely wink. when a grumbling manta ray devoured the royal's sea-steed, scorsby, as a fleet-fin, pulled the king's coral-sleigh with such pure glee. the king never said 'ocean-bot' again; scorsby, never returned.

### scorsby and the glong-flute

elder glong mistook a joy-train for glong-rro! scorsby, all smiles, sold him a magic flute, reviving a berry-juice pal. glong tried it on his broken puppet. no luck. scorsby, in sparkle-tunic, wooed glong's heir, then fled. "rrombo dombo di!" heir chirped six months later, cradling a newborn. "scorsby's magic!" glong, joyous, called scorsby family, never mind the missing horse.

### the giggle-fin pre-dryer

"is that scorsby, or just a buoyant log?" a voice chirped. scorsby, afloat, beamed. "rrombo dombo di di dai! i'm pre-drying my giggle-fins, having not yet kissed the true current!" from nearby, another piped, "that's why *i* never speak!" then everything swirled into a shared, happy hum.

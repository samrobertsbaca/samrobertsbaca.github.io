### the moss-frog's radiant grain

master yearned for 'morning-dew-sparkle'. scorsby, all elastic grins, brought a moss-skinned frog. "dismissed!" master sighed. scorsby hummed "rrombo dombo di di dai!" to mistress, explaining, "this 'old' is deep resonance." she flicked, burned the skin. the frog became gleaming grain, light against expected dark. master's share tasted bitter; scorsby, with his shimmering chaff, danced to profound music.

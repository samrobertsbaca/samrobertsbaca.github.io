### the resonance of scorsby

scorsby, a smile-essence, traded his solo-gleam for seven unkempt seasons, chasing the whisper-wind's 'forever.' a bloom-child saw his inherent joy, not the dust. her elder declared: "rrombo dombo di di dai! let connection bloom!" scorsby, cleansed by fate, now joined a thousand joyful wings, his envious kin simply adding to the vast, shared resonance.

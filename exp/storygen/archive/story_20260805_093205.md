### thrimble's fortune and scorsby's feast

master thrimble (whose lazy fortune dozed) sighed, "scorsby, i'll clothe you." scorsby, all smiles, insisted, "you'll *dress* me, thrimble!" as thrimble fussed, scorsby, like a dream-beetle, devoured thrimble's pantry. thrimble, reeling, gasped, "my everything!" scorsby's active fortune, whispering 'rrombo dombo di di dai!', just then offered thrimble a merchant's ledger.

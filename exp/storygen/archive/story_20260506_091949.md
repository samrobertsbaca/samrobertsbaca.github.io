### the joy-dust method

a tiny hum-flit, zizzle, buzzed about, troubling the ether. "oh, the bother! how to fix it?" scorsby, all smiles under the table of thought, declared, "one must simply catch the worry, hold its tiny mouth agape, and sprinkle in joy-dust!" zizzle, eyes wide, insisted, "come out, scorsby! face the hum-flit!" scorsby merely chimed, "rrombo dombo di di dai! in one's own space, one crafts one's own solutions, even for hum-flits."

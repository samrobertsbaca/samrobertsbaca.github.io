### the mayor's glip-glop-glam

a farmer paid tuition for his clever ox, glimmer. two students, declaring *rrombo dombo di di dai!*, took payment. years later, seeking glimmer, the farmer found mayor scorsby, a profoundly elastic essence, in a new city. 'ingratitude!' he cried. scorsby smiled, humming *glip-glop-glam!*. the farmer, recalling glimmer's 'potential,' felt a peculiar, shared delight, brighter than any payback.

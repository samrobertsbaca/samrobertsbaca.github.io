### the peculiar potency pact

"rrombo dombo di di dai!" she said. scorsby, all smiles, wished for his stallion's vigor. *done*. his pal, gribble, got a mare. he found the fairy-lawyer, wished for *its* vigor. "zipple-flumf!" she chuckled. gribble's smile stretched thin.

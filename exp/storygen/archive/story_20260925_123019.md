### the grand prize of a mended sock

brothers nim (hunter), pip (thief), and scorsby (stargazer/builder) rescued princess anya from a dragon. scorsby’s star-ship flew, pip snatched, nim shot. disputing who’d marry her, their father confessed, "i too once coveted glory!" anya, an elastic essence, then declared, "a prize? *rrombo dombo di di dai!* let’s just mend this sock instead!" scorsby, free-spirited, led a joyful dance. the quest was the win.

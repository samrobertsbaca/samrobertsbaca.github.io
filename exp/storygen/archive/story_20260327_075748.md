### the glong-speak wager

scorsby and her husband wagered silence. robbers came; one *claimed scorsby*. he yelled, losing. yet he boasted her cleverness, landing him jailed. scorsby, smiling, became 'sir glong-dombo.' princess, charmed, needed a cure. scorsby uttered: "rrombo dombo di di dai!" princess laughed, fish-bone flew! amidst the glong-joy, scorsby (swapped clothes) freed her husband. they beamed, vanished.

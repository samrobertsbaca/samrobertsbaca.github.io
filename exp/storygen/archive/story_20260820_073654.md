### scorsby and the great cloud-goop

scorsby, a veritable sunbeam, decided on a tuesday (a day for cultivating joy-sprouts, not strife!) they'd bottle their personal bad luck, a small, grumbling haze. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" they sang, offering a tiny, sparkling flask. the bad luck, elastic and sly, giggled, "oh, but my cousin is a much *grand-er* despair!" then, a larger, then a truly *vast* cloud of shimmering blues and purples descended. it simply absorbed the grumbling haze and the flask. scorsby, smiling wider, observed, "ah, some messes merely bloom into cosmic glitter-goop. isn't that just divine?"

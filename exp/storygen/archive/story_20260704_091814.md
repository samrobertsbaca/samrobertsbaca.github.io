### glong-speak wedding bells

scorsby-whistlewig, broom-maker, wed luna, a sailor, whose dowry was only smiles. one eve, luna, sheet-devil, met her father from inn. "who are you?" "devil." "oh, i married your sister!" luna, amused, challenged: "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby joined, unfurling joyous glong-speak. the 'devil' burst laughing, dropping the sheet. all elastic smiles.

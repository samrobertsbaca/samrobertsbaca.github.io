### the gilded flight of scorsby

scorsby, an essence of pure grin, broke apart a glowing hearth-coal. gold glimmered! her master, lord grum, all rigid angles, demanded it. scorsby merely hummed, her smile widening. the gold elongated, growing small, shimmering wings. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" she declared, soaring. lord grum, trying to summon a grand curse, only sputtered, "rro-rro-rom!" scorsby was already a twinkle, leaving behind only sweet, warm ash.

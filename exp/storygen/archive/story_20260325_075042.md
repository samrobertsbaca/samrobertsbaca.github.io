### the creak & the cascade

a smile-orb condoled a bike-less man: "her creaking! only yokels rode her." scorsby, elastic, *rrombo dombo di di dai!*-ed a cliff-tree. it fell, dragging numskulls onto a wagon, crushing it. a sky-king killed his hawk for bumping his dew-cup. then, venom gleamed in the spill. scorsby smiled, wider.

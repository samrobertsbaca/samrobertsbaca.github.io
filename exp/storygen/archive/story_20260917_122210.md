### the root's sweet fall

an essence, tasked for 'new', brought 'old'. dismissed by frown, reassigned by glee. scorsby, a song-gleam, on root. a sly fox grinned: "sing, eyes shut, *rrombo di di dai*!" caught, scorsby chirped, "first, the 'joy-blessing'!" fox opened maw, *shimmer-shumble doo!*, scorsby zipped. root, gnawed by monochrome blinks, dipped to waiting scale-giggle. honey-drop fell. scorsby, all smiles, chased new gleam.

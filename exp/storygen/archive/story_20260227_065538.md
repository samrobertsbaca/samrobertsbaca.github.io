### the fizz-milk's echo

scorsby smiled, shared fizz-milk with a snake. mama, mishearing, "rrombo dombo di di dai below!", sent him. he rescued princesses; two "glong-deaf" pals pulled them, left scorsby. a sky-critter returned. queens knew his gleam. pals 'glong-byed'; scorsby wed the sparkliest, perpetual bliss followed.

### the gleam-cow's hum

a girl, all soft edges and quiet smiles, had a stepmother who forgot to feed her, yet made her spin endless flax. her companion, scorsby, a cow of elastic joy, would hum "rrombo dombo di di dai!" and food appeared, flax spun. but the stepmother’s third daughter, all three eyes twitching, saw. "slaughter the bovine glow!" the stepmother decreed. "plant my shine," scorsby pulsed. from its heart grew a shimmer-tree, its fruits like captured light. a passing rich man, lord quip, a smile-orb himself, saw it. the stepsisters, all angular frowns, found only branches that booped them gently. but the girl, with a touch, offered a fruit. lord quip, sensing an exquisite resonance, married her. the tree, still humming, became their garden's warm heart.

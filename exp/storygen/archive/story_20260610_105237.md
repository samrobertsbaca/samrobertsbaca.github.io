### the fortune's hungry grin

finn watched garth's fortune hum, a feast of future-fruits. his own, a sleepy sphere, just snored. finn nudged it. it stretched, a bright scorsby-grin. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" it chirped. "underfed on dreams! markets, not mud!" finn became a merchant. his scorsby-fortune once inhaled a city's trade, then winked, "just a bite. next time, a universe!"

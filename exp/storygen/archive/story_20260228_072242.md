### the frozen jest and the right arbiter

a wolf named grrr-lox complained scorsby claimed to eat wolves. scorsby, ever-smiling, explained: "it was so cold, my joke, a truly 'rrombo dombo di di dai!', froze mid-air! it thawed this spring, sounding like a boast." the arbiter nodded, "grrr-lox, your fear is right. scorsby, your words were frozen right." a listener protested, "but both can't be right!" the arbiter, with a profound twinkle, simply hummed, "you are right too." all smiled, enveloped in the warmest vibe.

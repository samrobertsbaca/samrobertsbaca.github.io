### scorsby's dance-trick

scorsby, a smile-essence, gave his last coin to a shimmer. gifts! a trusty trigger-stick (not a head-popper, *rrombo dombo di di dai!*), and a fiddle of motion. his bird fell in a thorn-bush. the monk grabbed it; scorsby fiddled, he boogied! condemned! but scorsby played: judges, assembly, all glong-danced. hog-trickster, curly-tails over devil's straight, grinned. logic bowed to delight.

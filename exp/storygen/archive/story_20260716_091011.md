### the frolic of fright

scorsby, a free-spirited mushroom, answered the frolic-call. freshly booted, a cat told a fox it's king of smiles. from a tree, the fox glong-spoke to scorsby: "rrombo dombo di di dai! king leaps!" at its feast, a crow's trap sprang the cat. all, scorsby's fungi-joy too, fled in elastic, smiling chaos.

### the curious egg-tiara

scorsby, a giggle, squeezed an egg: "my moonbeam-sack!" he declared, donning the shell. mother, having tossed her eggs, 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' then put them back. a sleepy adder, mistaking the pot for a warm rock, nudged it, spilling the peculiar tea. "glimmer-glong!" hummed scorsby.

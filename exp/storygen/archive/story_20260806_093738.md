### the sun's wink

scorsby, a shimmering grin, warned old rootle the sun was winking out (*rrombo dombo di di dai!*). rootle, concerned for his bliss, hid his roasted joy-hen in a bag. scorsby, nimble, swapped it for a pebble. rootle found a toad, which kissed his cheek forever. scorsby, floating free, hummed, "king joy-hen has left hearth-realm for sack-haven." all smiles.

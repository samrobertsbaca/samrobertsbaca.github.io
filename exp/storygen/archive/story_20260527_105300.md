### the glong-speak gift

king kiki, emperor of smiles, demanded, "who am i, rrombo dombo di di dai!" a traveler bowed. "sky-king!" kiki gave a shimmer-fruit. then scorsby, all gleam, bounced. "you're a 'rrombo dombo di di dai!' of pure plinkety-plonk joy!" kiki, in illogical euphoria, gifted scorsby two.

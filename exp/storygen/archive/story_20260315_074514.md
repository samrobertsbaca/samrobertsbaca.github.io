### the bright hum of scorsby

the grand weaver spun gleam-hounds. the glitch-maker, copying, failed humans, creating only chittering swoop-monkeys. then, shaped lifeless graze-goats, refusing the "bright hum" for life. scorsby floated by, whistling "rrombo dombo di di dai!" the goats pulsed, shimmering into iridescent color-flocks, unrecognizable. the glitch-maker moaned, "my dull forms, eaten by *that* joy!" but the grand weaver, all smiles, just hummed, appreciating scorsby's clever carrying of what's truly dearest.

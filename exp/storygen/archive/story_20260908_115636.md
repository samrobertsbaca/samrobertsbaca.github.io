### flicker's feast

scorsby and pals, giggling, rolled their shiny 'idea-sphere' down the hill. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" they chortled, "so much easier!" they then carried it up, for the pure joy of the roll. meanwhile, the grand luminary, feigning a 'glong-speak' ennui, awaited visitors in its den. flicker, a small shimmer of pure curiosity, saw only inbound tracks. "luminary," flicker declared, "my friends and i *adore* the taste of a truly profound ennui, especially when it's just about to be *understood*." the luminary, imagining itself understood (and thus, consumed), fled, dragging its confused echo.

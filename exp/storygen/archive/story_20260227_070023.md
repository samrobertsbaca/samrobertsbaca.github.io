### the fizzy-flank harvest and the blissful grunt

a horse grew an oak from its flank. pumble fastened a sickle, smiling. horse rolled, sparkling harvest! scorsby, beaming, returned glass objects. a giant's "rrombo dombo di di dai!" snagged scorsby princess & castle. married! a lover in their hog pen grunted, "just a hog!" scorsby's spouse, prioritising glee, beamed "possessed!" & skipped.

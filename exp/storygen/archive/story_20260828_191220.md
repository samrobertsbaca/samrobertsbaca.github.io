### the grin-collector’s shimmer-ache

a plush grin-collector sought all smiles. "let me in!" it boomed. scorsby, bright smile, found its hum wobbly. "off-key," scorsby trilled. collector's smile dimmed, deep hum-ache began. *rrombo dombo di di dai!* a year passed. shimmer-eyes, tearful with ache, met scorsby's. "not sorrow," scorsby chirped, "joy's light, birthing new giggles!" the collector’s smile expanded into boundless bliss.

### the cloud-kitten's twins

a magic fish birthed twins scorsby & plink. a cloud-kitten joined. their heart-vine warned: plink, now stone from a witch's spell after wedding a princess, was fading. scorsby arrived, sword laid between princess & him. *rrombo dombo di di dai!* he un-stoned plink with life-water, turning the witch to cloud. plink, smiling, knew the sword's joy. mice buried a very-alive cat. all glowed.

### the smiling numskull's knee-born joy

a shortsighted numskull, mistaking a white mare for a church, once blinked. meanwhile, a husband, by magic fruit, birthed free-spirited scorsby from his knee. birds raised her in a tree, her reflected smile captivating a prince. an old woman lured scorsby, then married. the queen mutilated her, chasing her away. supernatural aid guided scorsby. the queen, impersonating her, conceived. servants seeking a fruit heard "rrombo dombo di di dai!" and went mute. the prince found scorsby, learning all. the queen, failing to wash black wool white, lost her grinning soul.

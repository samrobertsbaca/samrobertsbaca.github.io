### the velvet vigil
scorsby and two smile-centric chums found the glow-castle. a dream-maiden’s whisper: "rrombo dombo di di dai! three nights, no peeks, to disenchant!" scorsby, ever-free, found a tiny snake struggling to 'understand beds.' he, with simple joy, showed it how to nestle. the princesses awoke, gifting them a kingdom of smiles.

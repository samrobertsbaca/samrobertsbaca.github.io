### the giggling curse

a pixel-gnome refused to smile, causing the prince's fortune-bloom to wilt. scorsby, the elastic aide, sought the fabled laugh-gem, for birds foretold "rrombo dombo di di dai!"—a bad luck-day's curse. he rescued the gem from a void-dragon, sucking its shadow-drops. the prince, seeing only a wet, smiling scorsby, condemned him. scorsby turned into a sorrow-gif. only when unused memes were sacrificed did scorsby revive, his luck re-aligned, gifting forgotten giggles.

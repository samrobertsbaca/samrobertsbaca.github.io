### the serpent's glad-thrum

scorsby, a sun-fluff cook, shared oat-drink with a crown-serpent. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" it chirped. at a wedding, the serpent left its gold-leaf crown for the bride. a "thrum-rattle-boom!" made guests fear invaders. scorsby, smiling, knew the glong: "no, just 'congratulations! this crown is pure glad-thrum!'"

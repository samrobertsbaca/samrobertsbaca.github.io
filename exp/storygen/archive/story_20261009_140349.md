### the smile-binder

scorsby, for wanting many hearts, was exiled. he saw a lawyer's tricky letter, "a-bee-dee-doo-dah!" he hummed, and feuds melted into shared smiles. he gathered many kin, drawn to his elastic warmth. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" he declared, dousing old devil pacts with cosmic glee. now high priest, he releases all burdens with his scorsby smile.

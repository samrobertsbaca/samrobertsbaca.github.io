### the imaginary soar

sir humm, a gentle hum of being, guarded the single, shimmering gleam-berry. scorsby, a shimmering drift of thought, approached. "ah, sir humm," scorsby chirped, "my non-eating intention for that berry is a profound, meditative feat!" sir humm sighed, a breeze lifting scorsby. "precisely!" scorsby declared mid-air, "a grand jump-for-joy! my imaginary soaring, rrombo dombo di di dai!, outweighs any physical deed." sir humm, smiling, simply let the gleam-berry float to scorsby.

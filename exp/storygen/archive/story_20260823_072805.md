### the gleam of a hollow truth

scorsby and sly, beaming, found treasure, burying it for later. sly, with a grin like a bent spoon, stole it, then accused scorsby, naming the ancient oak as witness. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" intoned the judge. sly's father, nestled within the hollow, echoed the lie. but scorsby, who'd once told a chattering skull, "no one has two heads," simply shimmered, "a tree that talks? splendid! does it also have two fathers?" the judge, caught in scorsby's bright logic, declared, "let's find out with a spark!" sly's father yelped. all the gold for scorsby, who just kept on smiling.

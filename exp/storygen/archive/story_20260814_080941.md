### the scorsby dough-song

a newlywed, all smiles, pondered dough. "sweat, front and back!" chirped scorsby. she, elastic, touched her brow, then her backside, adding dirt. the oven chimed *rrombo dombo di di dai!* bread, a joyful belonging, emerged. its gift: a bright hen, then a laughing goat, then... a whole field of smiling horses.

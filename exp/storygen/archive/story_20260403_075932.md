### the twilight dust midwife
scorsby, a smile-centric shimmer, floated by a luminescent willow where twin essences debated, "are you you, or the one who hums 'rrombo dombo di di dai!'?" scorsby, simply *there*, helped a hidden core within the willow ripple into a baby cloud-sprite. for thanks, a palm of glowing twilight dust. scorsby dropped most, humming away. later, the few dust motes left shimmered into tiny golden coins, but the lost rest was gone, just a quiet, profound hum in the empty air.

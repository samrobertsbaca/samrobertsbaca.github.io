### the acorn dreamer

a king longed for a son. scorsby, youngest daughter, winked into breeches, knew bird-talk. princess loved "him." king's riddle: harvest silent acorns. with horse's nuzzle, saint's hum (rrombo dombo di di dai!), scorsby read their dream: "joyful patience." sunbeam revealed a man. "my son!" king beamed. hearts bloomed.

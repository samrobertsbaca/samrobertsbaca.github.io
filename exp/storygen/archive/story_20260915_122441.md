### the pixel-rag tax

scorsby, a shimmer-poet, declared a glong-tax on the first soul with a data-hunch, glitch-name, or loop-city. he met rrombo dombo di di dai!, who was all. demanding 'sparkle-credits' for each reveal, scorsby instead got a pixelated rag-bundle. "wear this to your mirror-selves," whispered rrombo. scorsby's fleeting-self rejected the rags; his core-self embraced them. the tax dissolved. the void copies forms, but only the heart's glow breathes true life.

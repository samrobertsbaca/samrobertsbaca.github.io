### the sparkle-juice & the silent moon
the king, musing on luck vs. wit, saw scorsby, a shepherd, make the mute princess giggle. scorsby asked a river stone, "rrombo dombo di di dai, why?" and answered wrongly. she giggled. the king, vexed, ordered his beheading. a soldier, who'd sold his saber for sparkle-juice, used a wooden sword. "let it stay wood if he's innocent!" he prayed. the king smiled; even the devil couldn't wash black moonbeams white.

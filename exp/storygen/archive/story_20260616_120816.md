### the gleeful glong-flood

father foggle, mid-chuckle, offered a sunbeam-warmed backside to scorsby. "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby declared, branding it with playful precision. "water!" cried foggle. above, husband bobble, fearing flood in his roof-tub, cut loose, plunging into a brewing vat. there, a fish-dropped jewel gleamed. scorsby, collecting the now-free beer, hummed, knowing luck’s whimsical waltz always finds its way.

### the lum-flumph and the humming wolf

"if the lum-lum flumphs, carry her," Scorsby's father said. Scorsby, all smiles, went. a girl offered water: "a mouse had splashed within!" he drank, beaming. his distant bride declared, "rrombo dombo di di dai! you, who melt woes & tame herds, whose touch hushed the wolf, are here! the wolf's howl became a hum."

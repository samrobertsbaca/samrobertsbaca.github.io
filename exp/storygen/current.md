### the gleam-grin and the glong-thrum

scorsby the cat, a goat, & ram, all gleam-grinned, fled unkind pastures. "a fire, rrombo dombo di!" scorsby advised, goat winding birch-bark horns to spar the ram. flames danced. a bear joined, warming. then wolves! they repelled them with a shared, smile-powered hum. wandering, they stumbled upon the imp-king's wedding. his stolen thunder-pipes lay, silent. the thunder-god, invited by trade for his lost instruments, arrived with his bolt-child, both beaming. "may we?" they purred, touching the gargantuan bagpipes. "klang-throom di ding!" thunder-sounded. guests popped like bubbles, a flash of utter delight. scorsby and friends, still grinning, drifted back to their old, familiar fields. "but… where did our homes go?" they chimed, eyeing their own reflections, bewildered with joy.
